Instructions

Please read the instructions carefully

General Instructions:

  1. Total duration of examination is 180 minutes.
  2. The clock will be set at the server. The countdown timer in the top right corner will display the remaining time. When the timer reaches zero, the exam ends automatically.
  3. The Question Palette on the right shows the status of each question using the following symbols:
You have not visited the question yet.
You have not answered the question.
You have answered the question.
You have NOT answered but marked for review.
The question is answered & marked for review.

The “Marked for Review” status simply indicates that you would like to look at that question again.

Navigating to a Question:

  1. Click a question number in the Question Palette to go directly to that question. (This does not save the current answer.)
  2. Click Save & Next to save your answer and move to the next question.
  3. Click Mark for Review & Next to save your answer, mark it for review, and go to the next question.

Answering a Question:

Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
  1. Click on an option to select your answer.
  2. Click again or Clear Response to deselect.
  3. Click another option to change your answer.
  4. Must click Save & Next to save your answer.
  5. Click Mark for Review & Next if you want to revisit the question.
  6. To change an already answered question, select it and follow the same procedure.

Navigating Through Sections:

  1. Sections appear in the top bar. The current section is highlighted.
  2. After the last question in a section, you will automatically be taken to the first question of the next section.
  3. You can shuffle between sections and questions anytime within the exam duration.
  4. Section summary is visible above the question palette.

Images in Questions:

  1. Roll over images to zoom in.
