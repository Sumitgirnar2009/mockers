Scheme of Examination for MHT CET

The details of the examination scheme related to the MHT CET are mentioned below for your reference.

  • The MHT CET is conducted online (Computer Based Test) for Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology at various centers across the state and outside Maharashtra.
  • You may appear in one or both examinations. Once selected, the choice of exam cannot be changed.
  • Questions are based on the syllabus prescribed by the State Council of Educational Research and Training Maharashtra.
  • No negative marking. The difficulty level is similar to JEE Mains and NEET, with application-based questions.
  • The exam includes 3 question papers of multiple-choice questions, each paper carrying 100 marks.
Paper Subject No. of Questions (Std. XI + Std. XII) Marks per Question Total Marks Duration

The exam medium is English, Marathi, and Urdu for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology. For Mathematics, the medium is English only. Exam centers are available across Maharashtra and selected neighboring states.
